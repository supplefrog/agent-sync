# Review and resolve one backlog item

Use this for an explicitly commissioned backlog assessment and repair; teams and evaluation tooling are optional means. Optimize useful outcomes and whole-job cost; an observed quality improvement can justify extra tokens. Reuse native bounded delegation, the current evaluator and the item's existing handoff. Use dynamic-workflows only when supported durable execution/resume is needed. This procedure is not a new scheduler or admission route.

## Assign the work

One coordinator owns the item, its outcome, current revision, decision and next action. Before dispatch, inspect active owners and uncertain operations; reconnect to an existing actor rather than duplicate it. Independent items may progress concurrently. Concurrent Git writers use separate worktrees. Workers producing candidate artifacts may instead use separate task-local directories outside the shared checkout. Give each writer exclusive paths. Read-only reviewers may share a frozen artifact. A shared evaluator/library defect has one repair owner; dependent items wait for a version-bound handoff rather than patching separate incompatible copies. Shared grants remain with their existing owner and reservation mechanism. No provider launch follows from a plan or a grant ID alone: the evaluator owner must confirm isolation, the applicable reservation and launch authority first. An unknown reservation remains unreserved.

## Diagnose both sides before spending

Assess the actual request, intended final output, constraints, exact artifacts and decisive evidence. Clarify a genuinely missing outcome before comparison spending. Reuse an existing reviewer judgment when it covers that outcome and those artifacts; obtain independent review when risk, uncertainty or author bias makes it useful, under the standing dispatch rules. Check both the skill/tool and any relevant evaluation: could the task expose the claimed advantage, do tools and orchestration remain available, do assertions follow user requirements, can valid alternatives pass and broken behavior fail, and are loading/accounting observations adequate? Diagnose a failed task before blaming either side. The coordinator checks actionable findings against source. An invalid evaluator result cannot justify retention, retirement or another trial with the same defect.

## Repair the cause, then evaluate if needed

Assign only a concrete repair: skill/tool owner for the capability defect, evaluator owner for the measurement defect. Fix deterministic defects with a direct reproduction and a relevant boundary check before another inference run. Keep the original observations and scores. Rebind affected evidence to the new source/oracle/runtime; don't reuse a historical pass after its relevant identity changes. Changes in both sides require separable snapshots so the comparison does not confound the skill change with an evaluator change.

Use evaluation tooling when controlled comparison, reproducibility, artifact binding or honest accounting is actually missing; it is not a mandatory separate judge or team. For comparative benefit, apply the existing claim-based testing and retirement contract and obtain matched evidence that can test the claim, with predeclared outcomes, adequacy/comparability, controls, bounds and stopping condition. Unsupported runtime fidelity is a hold on that claim, not permission to simplify away its mechanism. Source-only diagnosis can finish without models. Requested capability installation follows its actual authorized acceptance, not an invented comparative claim. Track root, worker, review, support, failed-attempt and correction cost; unobserved totals remain unknown. Do not keep spending just to turn a checklist green.

## Return to review and close the item

Check the requested usable outcome against the exact repairs, evidence and remaining limits. Reuse sufficient existing judgment; request further independent review under the standing review contract only when a material unresolved issue warrants it. Record retain, repair, retire or hold with the claim scope and reasons. Comparable valid outcomes without the skill support retirement under the existing contract; missing evidence is not comparability. A deterministic ownership correction is not a model-benefit finding. The coordinator accepts or resolves findings and records the justified disposition and next permitted action in the existing handoff. A reviewer recommendation alone cannot deploy or retire anything. Apply only authorized changes through the normal reconciler and verify its real targets; proposal, publication and installation remain distinct outcomes.

Move to the next independent item when the current one is resolved or has an exact named dependency. Retain the blocked item with its resumption event, evidence identities and owner; don't poll, respawn or repeat a denied action without new relevant evidence. No fixed fan-out, trial count, universal threshold or mandatory reviewer for tiny unrelated edits is introduced.
