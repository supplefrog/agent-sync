# Reference-led first drafts

Use after selecting the reference and before constructing its first implementation. Inspect the actual image, including a model-generated mockup; its generation prompt does not establish what the image depicts. Routine repairs inherit approved interpretations and need only the assumptions affected by the repair.

## Show the construction assumptions

State a compact interpretation to the user before implementation: the defining shapes, relative proportions, materials and palette roles, spatial relationships, and intended behavior. Include consequential interpretations even when confident; do not rely on self-assessed uncertainty to decide what the user can inspect. Distinguish supplied requirements from inferred anatomy or behavior, and label estimated dimensions as estimates. Describe perceptible relationships rather than listing every CSS value or implementation detail.

The model writes this brief; do not ask the user to produce it. Alongside it, offer one or two short correction examples tailored to this reference during production. Examples might distinguish a wheel from a rounded pill, change housing-to-knob proportions, or specify that a bolt travels with its assembly. Use examples that help inspect the current image, not a generic worksheet or a prescribed aesthetic. Make the assumptions available early enough to redirect construction; continue independent authorized preparation without adding a compulsory approval round. Resolve material taste choices through the existing taste-selection procedure, and do not treat silence as approval.

## Build relationships into the construction

Use the brief to choose a representation capable of the defining silhouette and behavior. Encode relationships in shared geometry, moving groups and lifecycles, palette roles, or signal/control ownership rather than correcting each part independently. Establish the defining component and any consequential motion in context before extending its treatment across the page. Reuse approved constructions where they fit the current idea without transferring another project's style wholesale. Apply the existing material-reconstruction and motion-design guidance for the relevant cases.

## Check a bounded first draft

Prioritize the whole composition and the few relationships that define recognition. Compare them against the actual reference. Observe relevant intermediate motion, entry/exit, reversal and interruption, not just endpoints; verify that associated parts remain coherent, intended content persists, and clipping or discontinuities do not appear. Use the existing acceptance record and required checks rather than inventing a second checklist or reviewer loop.

Fix observed defects within the existing scope and attempt ceiling. A recurring failure calls for reconsidering the representation or exposing the unresolved choice in a local lab, not another general aesthetic self-critique cycle. Preserve working qualities. This bounded pass does not replace delivery acceptance or human taste selection.

When testing this procedure, preserve the initial render before correction and record the model, brief and active guidance. Judge whether the user needs fewer corrections and whether total time/cost improves; completed source checks or one successful draft do not establish a general quality benefit.
