# Liked motion and material options

These are reusable choices explicitly raised by this user, with the corrections that made them useful. Consult them when the product role or requested expression fits. They are not a mandatory animation pack, shape palette or single style. Show selected parts before implementing them when requested; [component-selection.md](component-selection.md) owns discovery and [motion-design.md](motion-design.md) owns playback checks. Exact project assets, colors, timings and approved treatments stay local.

| Choice | Useful relationship | Preserve or verify |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal card travel followed by an image zoom | Exploring artifacts or a visual collection; movement establishes which object becomes the focus | Let lateral travel settle enough to read before the zoom; tune any brief pause to the distance and input. Preload the real image, keep it visible inside its intended frame, and clip every intermediate state to the rounded boundary. No exposed placeholder, rectangular spill into neighbors or competing transform owners. Test reverse travel and zoom interruption. |
| Text or textbox highlight sweep | Finding, selecting or emphasizing an actual passage; a sentence-fitting expressive reveal can also sell an idea | Keep the words readable and the text/box treatment coordinated. Control the trigger and reset; do not add the same fade/sweep to every heading. [Fancy Text Highlighter](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/text/text-highlighter) is a source candidate for inline highlighting, not a complete textbox sweep. |
| Animated underline or strip | A mark being drawn, a collection progressing or a thematic band whose motion contributes to the idea | Choose the correct origin/direction and sensible speed; preserve readable content and pause/reduced-motion behavior. [Fancy underline](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/text/underline-animation) and [marquee](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/blocks/simple-marquee) are candidates to inspect, not required treatments. |
| Cursor and sentence-fitting text reveal | Editing, writing or a deliberate reveal where typing/revealing conveys something | Use timing that suits the sentence. Avoid jitter, shifting line breaks, repeated entrance choreography and forced waiting for ordinary reading or input. |
| Small floating or tilting artifacts | Lightweight notes, objects or cards whose material and action make movement believable | Modest lift/tilt can express manipulability or delight. A large screenshot floating while its image zooms needs a separate justification; inspect the composite, rather than copying both attractive effects. |
| Moving selector and restrained glow | Make selection, location or a newly formed relation perceptible | Couple the indicator with the actual selected content. A moving bar alone does not finish the transition. Maintain connected diagram geometry at all sizes. |
| Reader paper texture | A deliberately chosen reading surface that adds material character and visual comfort | This is an exception to automatic tinted-paper backgrounds because it has an explicit product role. Use authored or licensed texture; inspect fine detail, intensity, repetition, seams, dark-mode compositing and print. Do not claim reduced eye strain without evidence or tint every product. Native image generation is available under personal defaults. |
| Animated reader theme control | A control and surface change that remain understandable through light/dark switching | An icon morph and a whole-page reveal are different choices. Coordinate the control with surface colors without flashing; preserve state, contrast, reduced motion and rapid reversal. [Animate UI theme toggler](https://animate-ui.com/docs/components/buttons/theme-toggler) is a candidate for a theme transition, not proof of the user's preferred icon morph. |
| Complete opening and closing | Notes, panels, disclosures and details retain continuity instead of appearing abruptly | Cover both directions, content lifetime and rapid reversals across the requested element family. Keep frequent commands immediate; no universal animation quota. Existing accessible primitives can supply lifecycle/semantics while styling expresses the theme. |

Use these relationships to query suppliers or adapt an existing component. Some sequences are compositions of proven parts rather than one ready-made export. Reuse the suitable pieces and write only the missing coordination; verify the combined result. A library's polished demo does not establish smooth motion, accessibility or fit in the product.
