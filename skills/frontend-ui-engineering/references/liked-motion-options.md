# Liked motion and material options

These are reusable choices explicitly raised by this user. Consult them when the product role or requested expression fits. They are not a mandatory animation pack, shape palette or single style. Choosing whether to introduce an emphasis highlight is optional; once chosen, its fitting animation follows [personal-design-defaults.md](personal-design-defaults.md). Follow the standing parts-preview preference in [component-selection.md](component-selection.md), which owns discovery; [motion-design.md](motion-design.md) owns playback checks. Exact project assets, colors, timings and approved treatments stay local.

| Choice | Useful relationship | Preserve or verify |
|---|---|---|
| Text or textbox highlight sweep | Finding, selecting or emphasizing an actual passage; a sentence-fitting expressive reveal can also sell an idea | Keep the words readable and the text/box treatment coordinated. Control the trigger and reset; do not add the same fade/sweep to every heading. [Fancy Text Highlighter](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/text/text-highlighter) is a source candidate for inline highlighting, not a complete textbox sweep. |
| Animated underline or strip | A mark being drawn, a collection progressing or a thematic band whose motion contributes to the idea | Underlines can be expressive: a scribbled draw, curved arc, traveling wave or compressing bar. Inspect [Codrops LineHoverStyles](https://tympanus.net/Development/LineHoverStyles/), [Rough Notation](https://roughnotation.com/) or [Fancy underline](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/text/underline-animation); [marquee](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/blocks/simple-marquee) serves a different strip role. Choose the treatment and trigger to fit the text; preserve readability, wrapping, keyboard focus and reduced motion. None is required. |
| Cursor and sentence-fitting text reveal | Editing, writing or a deliberate reveal where typing/revealing conveys something | Use timing that suits the sentence. Avoid jitter, shifting line breaks, repeated entrance choreography and forced waiting for ordinary reading or input. |
| Small floating or tilting artifacts | Lightweight notes, objects or cards whose material and action make movement believable | Modest lift/tilt can express manipulability or delight. When a composition combines this with image zoom, inspect how the effects work together. |
| Expressive illustration response | A homepage illustration can invite attention or make its idea tangible through a one-time hook or something to explore | Options include restrained cursor parallax for spatial patterns, a drawn circle/mark/arrow, a material fold, or a wave around an anchored object. Choose the movement from the illustration's meaning rather than assigning the same effect to every hero. Decide whether it plays once, replays deliberately or responds to input; avoid endless ambient repetition by default. Keep important content and task access stable, and provide appropriate coarse-pointer, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior. Exact effects on particular comparison designs remain project choices. |
| Moving selector and restrained glow | Make selection, location or a newly formed relation perceptible | Couple the indicator with the actual selected content. A moving bar alone does not finish the transition. Maintain connected diagram geometry at all sizes. |
| Reader paper texture | A deliberately chosen reading surface that adds material character and visual comfort | This is an exception to automatic tinted-paper backgrounds because it has an explicit product role. Use authored or licensed texture; inspect fine detail, intensity, repetition, seams, dark-mode compositing and print. Do not claim reduced eye strain without evidence or tint every product. Native image generation is available under personal defaults. |
| Animated reader theme control | A control and surface change that remain understandable through light/dark switching | An icon morph and a whole-page reveal are different choices. [Toggles.dev Classic](https://toggles.dev/toggles/classic) and [Expand](https://toggles.dev/toggles/expand) change shared SVG geometry into a crescent; [Animate UI theme toggler](https://animate-ui.com/docs/components/buttons/theme-toggler) is a page-transition candidate. Choose either or both only as the product calls for them. Preserve theme state, contrast, reduced motion and rapid reversal; verify the actual morph rather than inferring one from an animated icon swap. |
| Complete opening and closing | Notes, panels, disclosures and details retain continuity instead of appearing abruptly | Cover both directions, content lifetime and rapid reversals across the requested element family. Keep frequent commands immediate; no universal animation quota. Existing accessible primitives can supply lifecycle/semantics while styling expresses the theme. |

Slides and image zooms are valid standalone choices; there is no preference that zoom follow a slide. When a design combines them, check their coordination, interruption and reversal without imposing a fixed order or pause. Preserve the intended frame during movement; verify rounded clipping only when that frame is rounded, and do not introduce rounding as a repair rule. These are conditional correctness checks, not liked effects or required features.

Use these relationships to query suppliers or adapt an existing component. A suitable premade component can stand alone. Add coordination only when the brief or chosen composition calls for combined parts, then verify that result. A library's polished demo does not establish smooth motion, accessibility or fit in the product.
