## Working sequence

Use the sequence flexibly. Read [references/defect-acceptance.md](defect-acceptance.md) and the affected families in [references/regression-catalog.md](regression-catalog.md) while planning interactive, responsive or motion-bearing work, before composing controls. Classify the retained requirements and selected wants in the existing acceptance record; do not start over from the latest criticism. Its coverage check blocks omitted applicable cases, stale evidence and lost commitments. Load other references when they resolve an actual decision. Keep engineering requirements distinct from aesthetic recipes. A skill/no-skill quality claim requires a comparable rendered test and the relevant user's judgment, not a source audit or anecdotes alone.

For iterative comparison or repair batches, choose one visible outcome, baseline and time or attempt ceiling before starting; use the project's existing ceiling when available. Stop at that ceiling, or earlier when the same fundamental defect persists or process work displaces product progress. Preserve the candidate and present the improvement, remaining failure and stop/change decision; do not automatically start another batch. Routine one-off repairs need no separate budget document.

Keep the chosen variant/settings, exact source revision or file hashes, remaining defects and relevant checks in the existing handoff or design record. Update that record after selection or transfer; preserve open findings and distinguish selected, repair-verified and finished status. Verify transferred code and changed assets against the chosen version before presenting the receiving preview. Use existing transfer helpers only when movement between environments needs them.

After edits, recheck affected behavior and nearby valid cases. Reuse unaffected evidence only while its relevant source and runtime dependencies still match; record the scoped justification. Invalidate changed claims without restarting unrelated collection. Scoped reuse does not establish a current whole-product pass or replace missing evidence.

1. **Inspect and frame.** Read the repository, running UI, design tokens, assets, stack, and relevant tests. Establish audience, task, real content, primary action, constraints, and required states. Before choosing controls, map each necessary user job to its access path, feedback and recovery on the relevant desktop and phone layouts. Retain multiple controls for a job only when their different contexts justify them. Bind accepted qualities and the requested change to representative views and states; make preservation observable rather than asking a worker to keep the vibe. Understand the audience-specific meaning of existing humor or imagery before replacing it with generic whimsy; ask when that meaning is unclear. A local component change inherits its surrounding system and skips whole-page concept work.
2. **Set the direction.** For a new visual identity or major redesign, follow the image-led workflow in `references/design-judgment.md`: whole-page worlds, selected master, related real layouts and consequential states. Resolve how the product idea becomes visible and, where useful, interactive. Compare requested alternatives before committing them to a common visible template. An approved identity or local repair retains its references and surrounding direction.
3. **Compose the experience.** Work out content relationships, responsive transformations, state changes and assets together. Plan an action's visible response at its actual use position, and an expressive element's relationship to its neighbors in the states where it matters. Read `references/motion-design.md` while planning an interactive or expressive demonstration, before choosing whether and how it moves. Integrate the explanation or demonstration into the chosen idea rather than appending a stock app window. Render the most consequential or uncertain relationship with representative content early enough to change its structure before expanding it across the page. For refinement or repair, preserve order unless changing it is requested or necessary to resolve the named defect; authorized redesign may establish a new order. Routine UI needs no compulsory signature effect. Create or expand project `DESIGN.md` only if requested or the in-scope work establishes a multi-page or reusable system.
4. **Implement with the project.** Reuse its framework, components, tokens, icons, and dependencies. Preserve routes, behavior, factual copy, and unrelated work. Check source, license, compatibility, accessibility, performance, and token fit before importing external components. Generation, paid services, account-bound assets, uploads, and deployment need their applicable authorization.
5. **Render, diagnose, refine.** Capture the baseline before changing an existing surface and compare with the same content, viewport and state. Examine purpose and repetition separately from painted layout and complete interaction; a clean result in one does not establish the others. Use the selected cases from `references/defect-acceptance.md`, including local boundaries and changed states where relevant. Fix causes without discarding accepted qualities to make a check pass. If a repair changes copy, controls, structure, constraints or motion, compare every affected commitment again, including those outside the named defect. Change an expectation only for an explicit brief or supported design reason, then recheck it.
6. **Verify delivery.** Bind build/tests, browser findings and visual judgments to the delivered revision. At export and closeout, consume current coverage through the delivery lane in [references/coverage-schema.md](coverage-schema.md), retaining previous contracts and findings on revisions. For new builds and recurring consequential misses, map user jobs and state edges from the brief and actual routes, controls and state producers to existing cases; collect ordinary running journeys and current fresh artifact-first scrutiny that also discovers omitted paths. Whole-product readiness requires that extent; a passing affected repair remains `repair_verified`. Transport and recovery remain available with acceptance false. For approved-reference work, use its reference-bound comparison packet: inspect the whole composition and consequential components in context, with explicit findings for the relationships a repair could disturb. A green measurement report cannot clear a failed or missing rendered comparison. Resolve known defects and material verification gaps before calling the result ready for taste review or committing. Exploratory demos may expose unresolved choices with their status explicit. User review selects taste; it does not substitute for defect acceptance. Stop once the brief and independent commitments hold; do not restart unrelated aesthetic choices.

Read `references/webpage-workflow.md` when classifying supplied references, recovering or importing assets, importing components, or creating a requested/in-scope design-system document. Before choosing external sources, components, or extraction tools, read `references/resource-catalog.md`. During authorized skill-integration work, compare unique behaviors and extend this owner rather than installing overlapping frontend triggers; `references/provenance.md` records the reviewed sources, decisions, and evidence limits.
