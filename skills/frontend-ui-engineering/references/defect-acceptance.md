# Defect acceptance before taste review

Use this for interactive, responsive or motion-bearing changes. Keep the record in the project's existing requirements, test or handoff artifact. A simple repair needs only its affected cases; a whole-page build needs the user journeys and their responsive transformations. Use the project's mature tests and accessible primitives before adding machinery.

## Choose cases before composing controls

Start from the skill's acceptance contract: what the person must understand or accomplish, the intended expression, and the accepted qualities a change could disturb. Keep these independent rather than trading layout, useful content and interaction against an overall impression. For each affected job, identify its use position, access path, visible response and recovery in the relevant input/layout contexts. This exposes duplicate navigation and unreachable actions before they become widgets. Different access paths can be useful when their contexts differ; do not remove them solely because they share a destination. Carry these commitments and cases through delegation and subsequent revisions in the existing project record.

Record the important behavior promises as observable relationships, with the scenario and an appropriate tolerance: a trigger stays anchored while its disclosure opens; a reading action is usable at the reading position; each valid activation produces the intended state; a panel finishes without an unplanned geometry jump. When one task has several representations, check their agreement after settlement: the reached destination, current indication and adjacent actions must describe the same context. Observe the visible result rather than treating a handler call, changed URL or rotating icon as the whole response. Intentional movement, deferred actions and responsive rearrangement need their own expectations. Do not turn these examples into universal layout rules.

For persistent navigation, specify where the reader starts, where the opened content actually lands, and how they return. Test from the middle and bottom of the real content on the phone, not only the fresh page at scroll zero. A header control that opens an offscreen document section has not exposed its contents. Choose a fitting anchored panel, navigation move or other usable access path during composition; no single drawer or sidebar layout is required. Link the use-position case to the existing `reachable_action` measurement when rectangular hit geometry is appropriate, and retain the rendered finding.

Choose representative content and stress cases capable of falsifying those promises. Select by rendering mechanism as well as task: relevant cases include the first/last item and scroll extremes of a local scroller, focused fields beside their labels, wrapped control rows, changing values with different digit/decimal widths, empty-to-one-item content, replacement content and expanded/editing states. A changing value must not move controls promised to stay anchored; numeric text and native indicators need actual painted alignment and usable room. Markers, strokes and handles must meet their intended boundaries, including pseudo-elements. Content-count transitions need deliberate sizing and surrounding layout behavior. A fixed chassis, monitor or task surface retains its declared size across mode, count and connection changes; variable content uses deliberate reserved space, scrolling or another fitting internal layout. Compare real content and controls before and after on desktop and phone; frozen blank space cannot establish a working state transition. Neither fixed heights nor zero movement are universal requirements. Include the actual long list or answer, relevant container widths and scrollbar occupancy when these affect the changed behavior. A tiny demonstration cannot establish acceptance of the full-content surface. Freeze expected behavior before collecting results; do not weaken it to make a failed implementation pass. Change an expectation only for a documented brief or design reason, then rerun affected cases.

## Collect evidence from the experience

Exercise the complete task with ordinary supported input. Check the surrounding page as well as the isolated component. For state changes, observe trigger, content, neighboring layout and destination through entry, exit, interruption/reversal and final settlement, plus relevant keyboard, touch and reduced-motion paths. Follow focus during exit, not only after it: outgoing content must not offer clipped or hidden interaction, and an affected focus must land on a meaningful visible control. Check the next Tab or activation while closure is underway and after settlement. Preserve interaction on reversal; do not remove animation or hardcode one implementation merely to pass. Inspect motion at normal speed and inspect intermediate frames when needed to locate a defect. Separate control rotation from movement of its entire container. Include the actual reading or use position; locator auto-scrolling can conceal an inaccessible action.

For consequential expressive motion, record what the ordinary interaction appears to change and which relationship its playback suggests, then compare that reading with the design prediction and actual state/data. When separate inspection is useful, obtain this reading before the author's rationale. Check whether the important change remains perceptible while the effect attracts attention; the presence or smoothness of an effect cannot establish its meaning. A contradiction is a defect; unclear required feedback remains unresolved. Expressive ambiguity can remain a taste choice when the task and represented relationships stay legible. Keep this finding in the existing motion case rather than adding a compulsory reviewer or prescribing an effect.

Include relevant ways state can change outside the component's usual click handler: native fragment navigation, browser back/forward, disclosure expansion, system settings and restored state. After such entry, exercise the next ordinary action against the state the person actually sees. For overlays, test that scroll/pan stays in the intended layer and that closing preserves the surrounding task position. Browser modality alone does not establish scroll preservation. Select these cases by the affected capability, not as a compulsory matrix for every component.

Inspect the painted result in context, not only layout boxes or document width. Local overflow, masks and animated containers can clip focus outlines, selection halos, glyphs, strokes or content while the page has no horizontal overflow and the target remains clickable. Check relevant paint at the boundary and state where it extends furthest, including focused controls beside labels and revealed content before settlement. For page overflow, compare scrollable width with the document's available client width, accounting for scrollbar occupancy; `innerWidth` can include space unavailable to content. Record horizontal scroll position as well: automatic scrolling to a focused target can conceal the original overflow. Preserve intentional crops and local scrolling when their content and feedback remain usable; do not hide overflow or remove focus to obtain a pass. Compare related controls and text/icon groups by their visible balance, insets and relationship to neighbors. Optical placement is a rendered judgment, not a requirement to give different roles identical heights or center every composition mathematically.

Bind observations to the build/revision or file hash, browser, viewport, content fixture, starting state, input and capture method. For a live renderer, collect the affected object at its actual scrolled use position after initialization; an offscreen object's retained metadata or a correct DOM value does not establish fresh paint. Verify loaded resource identity and contemporaneous renderer/anchor dimensions when those are at issue, then inspect the capture for fallback, clipping and stale pixels. Retain raw geometry/event records or recordings behind the finding. Measure browser facts rather than asking a model to guess coordinates, hit targets or capture completeness. Keep unsupported checks unresolved and distinguish emulation from physical-device evidence.

Use the affected families in [regression-catalog.md](regression-catalog.md) to classify requirements, conditional defects, selected capabilities and uncertain observations before implementation. Keep this classification with the existing brief; the catalog supplies expectations, not another project brief or an effect quota. Compare declared features with the actual rendered controls so a missing feature tag cannot hide a check. Add project-specific requirements alongside these cases when the brief needs them. Retain the classification through handoffs and revisions; changing copy does not cancel geometry checks, and repairing geometry does not cancel purpose or selected finishing treatments.

For repeated measurable checks, execute [../scripts/frontend_gate.py](../scripts/frontend_gate.py); [defect-gate-schema.md](defect-gate-schema.md) describes its four measurement kinds. Separately execute [../scripts/frontend_coverage.py](../scripts/frontend_coverage.py) with the catalog, selected contract and revision-bound findings, as described in [coverage-schema.md](coverage-schema.md). Keep coverage and measurement status separate. Missing applicable pre-review cases, stale or absent pre-review evidence and dropped commitments block pre-review readiness; catalog-owned later human-choice, integration and publication cases remain pending and block full readiness; `promised: false` in the measurement checker cannot waive a required case. A rendered judgment needs the inspected render and a concrete finding. Neither checker collects evidence, validates aesthetic truth or certifies accessibility. Passing fixtures or authored policy do not establish a real UI pass.

For moving surfaces, retain separate evidence for continuity/reversal, active-window responsiveness, entry/reload lifecycle and repeated normal-speed smoothness (`I25`), and rapid-command retargeting plus real manual takeover of animated navigation (`I26`) when present. Entry evidence must cover actual initialization from first visibility through settlement; a warmed post-load replay cannot replace it. Record absence when there is no entry motion, without adding an effect or a compulsory startup profile. Exercise affected cases after integration and any repair that can change their cost or animation ownership. Final styles, correct anchors and source listeners cannot clear those independent cases; use the profiling and interruption guidance in `motion-design.md`.

For selectors, inspect idle, mouse-open/close/cancel/selection, keyboard focus/selection and mixed input. Capture the closed control after mouse use as well as the popup. A native popup's system highlight does not explain an authored halo on the closed trigger. Inspect the actual indicator's visible insets and pivot at narrow and normal sizes; CSS box centers alone do not establish optical balance. Keep keyboard and forced-colors focus visible, and meaningful selection distinguishable. Do not suppress all outlines or rebuild an accessible picker solely to pass a styling test.

## Review findings and accept

Classify findings by their evidence:

- **Defect:** a task, accessibility requirement or declared behavior fails; a visual explanation contradicts the state or relationship it is meant to convey. Reproduce it and fix the cause.
- **Taste:** several coherent, usable treatments meet the brief. Offer a scoped comparison when the choice is material and unresolved.
- **Unresolved:** intent, observation or coverage is insufficient to decide. Obtain the missing evidence or clarify the particular decision; do not silently count it as passed.

Interpret visual conventions in context. An upward caret can mean collapse while a list opens below; abstract theme motion need not imitate sunlight. When an effect deliberately depicts a source, direction or attachment, inspect whether the rendered sequence consistently conveys that relationship. A metaphor disagreement without an observable contradiction remains a taste or unresolved question.

For a whole-page image-led reconstruction, or a consequential composition/interaction that repeatedly passes narrow checks while receiving defect reports, use a fresh scrutiny reviewer under the standing dispatch contract. Supply the approved authority, current revision, reference-bound comparison packet, ordinary interaction path and retained acceptance contract before the author's explanation or test verdicts. The reviewer inspects the whole composition, component relationships and actual use; it should discover omissions as well as reproduce named defects. Record concrete findings in the existing cases, distinguishing defects, taste and unresolved evidence. The parent routes a bounded repair batch and reruns failing cases plus affected neighbors; fresh follow-up scrutiny is warranted for changed composition, representation or unresolved findings. Keep the result blocked while material defects or evidence gaps remain. Repeated failure without new evidence reopens the construction/resource choice rather than inviting another cosmetic patch. Stop when the retained contract holds, not when a reviewer sounds satisfied. Review does not supply human taste approval or certify universal defect freedom; small unambiguous repairs need no compulsory reviewer.

Keep each repair bounded by its reproduced cause and affected contract: retain raw findings separately from repaired-revision evidence, state the causal change, then rerun the failing case and affected neighbors/inherited commitments. If the same approach stalls or repeats the failure without new evidence, change the representation or implementation at the cause while preserving accepted content, access and expression; do not keep stacking cosmetic patches or reopen unrelated taste. Record the remaining blocker when the cause or required evidence cannot be established within the task's bounds.

Fix known defects and retest their causes, affected neighbors and inherited commitments. A geometry repair must retain useful content, action economy and expression; a content/action repair must retain accepted geometry, access and motion. Compare the final cases with the same accepted baseline and record any intentional change of expectation. Missing material evidence blocks readiness just as a known failure does. Label exploratory demos accordingly; do not hand a failed result to the user as ready for taste review. Report the revision, observed scope, remaining gaps and whether the independent commitments hold. Preserve the skill's separate rendered delivery and user approval requirements before visual commits. Stop when the task and acceptance criteria are met; no fixed pass count or endless reviewer loop is required.
